--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 A reading system for whether a person, group, institution, or design
 generates more than it consumes [True Coherence] or consumes while
 looking like it generates [False Coherence].

 extraction mechanisms [tricks that make prey help consume itself].

PLAIN VOCABULARY [used before codes]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 System: whatever you are reading [person, org, state, tool, design].
 Axis: the orientation the system steers by [values, purpose, identity].
 Substrate: what the system runs on [people, trust, attention, land,
 money, ecology] -- the fuel, not the slogan.
 Meter / measurement surface: how the system [or you] scores success.
 Deployer: who built or aims the mechanism.
 Capture: the meter, axis, or boundary is owned or bent by an extractor.
 Extraction: trickery where the prey cooperates with its own consumption
 [distinct from open competition or visible predation -- below].
 Weapon / mechanism: a named extraction pattern [not always literal force].
 Stack: several mechanisms co-deployed so each amplifies the others.
 Correction: detecting deviation and fixing it toward a real target.
 WIP: work-in-progress staging -- not kit truth until promoted.

EXTRACTION VS HONEST HARM [theory Section Two -- plain]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Cooperation: both sides gain inside the relationship.
 Competition: both know the terms; zero-sum declared.
 Predation: honest violence; boundary visible; kills mostly.
 Extraction: trickery; prey cooperates with its own consumption.

IDEOLOGY 1% AND 99% [plain -- not all creeds; bad run tell]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Many fights start from a **real row** [one victim, one injustice, one hard edge case --
 the **1%**]. That row can be **true** and still **not** justify everything sold with it.
 **Bad ideology run [not a good thing]:** use the true **1%** to **force the 99%** --
 whole package, whole category, whole jersey, perpetual outrage, valves [HR, law, platform,
 school] that voters would not pass if argued openly.
 **Good repair [classical-lib shape]:** fix the **named row** with a **pin** [what changes,
 who pays, when it ends]; do **not** merge guilt-by-bundle. Oppose the stack ≠ oppose the
 victim.
 **Tell:** debate keeps returning to the hardest story; the **typical case at scale** [what
 law and money actually hit] never gets argued on its own terms.
 **Detail:** disorder_forms **GUIDE -- IDEOLOGY-ACTIVATION-BAD** [ONE PASS]; theory **EDGE
 CASE UNIVERSALIZATION** [hard case genuine -- universalization is the weapon].

THREE CYCLES [how to read any system]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 C1 GENERATIVE CYCLE [GC]: does the system produce what it needs to keep
 running? [fuel, trust, competence, replacements]
 C2 MEASUREMENT: MEASUREMENT INTEGRITY [MI] + CAUSAL LEGIBILITY [CL] --
 can it tell what is true? is the meter captured? can harm be traced?
 C3 TIME: TRANSMISSION SELECTIVITY [TS] + SUCCESSOR AUTONOMY [SA] --
 what crosses to the next generation, and can successors revise it?
 Application layer [after cycles]: theory SCALE LADDER [minimum stacks +
 tension units]; RECOVERY TRAJECTORIES [intervention by interference type].

HONEST LIMITS T1-T9 [measurement ceilings -- theory Section Four]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Not moral failures -- structural ceilings on what a read can resolve.
 T1 RESOLVABLE: fully measurable from current position.
 T2 PRE-RESOLUTION: indeterminate until a stress event; named state,
 not a failed reading. Subtype A = concealment; B = variability.
 T3 ANALYST BIAS: the instrument cannot calibrate its own axis from
 inside; declare Low/Medium/High; run an adversarial check.
 T4 STRUCTURAL OBSCURATION: transfer direction hidden by design
 better analysis alone cannot pierce the weapon stack.
 T5 PRE-MEASUREMENT: productive state before a meter exists
 forcing measurement disrupts the state [flow, rest, skill].
 T6 OBSERVER EFFECT: measuring changes the system; uncorrectable.
 T7 FERTILE LOCK: self-sealing capture; internal correction may be
 structurally impossible -- external contact only.
 T8 RECURSIVE CLOSURE: observer = instrument = observed; disclosure
 makes recursion legible but does not remove it.
 T9 IRRATIONALITY MAINTENANCE: direct correction risks critical
 internal damage [CID = load-bearing identity/body/axis break]
 measure, document, act around -- not through.
 Do not treat T9 as T3 and apply standard correction.
 T9 SUBTYPES [all still T9]:
 Identity: admission breaks who you are [civic, diagnostic, role].
 Somatic: correction would destabilize the body [load-bearing pattern].
 Ideological: correction feels like axis betrayal not axis fix.
 Non-reception: read is accurate but receiver cannot absorb it
 experienced as attack -- gap is not informational.
 T9 INTERVENTION [when direct correction blocked]:
 Outcome comparison, historical distance, cost accounting
 [search T9 SOFT EXPOSURE METHODS in applied Section Five].
 SELF-CRITICAL vs T9 [close topic -- not the same tool]:
 Self-critical thought keeps GENUINE AXIS [GA] examined. T9 = admission
 would cause CID -- go around, not through. Error: demand "be more
 self-critical" from outside at a T9 load.

HONESTY [theory Section Two -- main epistemic standard]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Not moral praise or disclosure virtue. Operational standard policy
 tradeoffs must pass before they count as correction: both sides of the
 cost ledger on one method. Four tests: symmetric accounting; causal
 chain for solutions; no purity veto; one measurement method. CORE RULE:
 price the fix while hiding body count [or the reverse] = dishonest
 regardless of stated purpose.

SOLUTION VETO [theory Section Two -- named pattern]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Fix class forbidden as sin or maladaptation while preventable harm from
 the unfixed problem is tolerated. Veto-holders keep the tool; dead are
 old, poor, or voiceless. Tell: institution uses the tool for itself while
 prescribing passive coping for others = confirmed. HONESTY test [3].

NOT HONESTY vs HONEST LIMIT [disambiguation]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 Structural limits are NOT the honesty standard.
 HONEST LIMITS T1-T9 = measurement ceilings.
 HONEST LIMIT local = scope humility for targeting.
 MASS ADOPTION HONEST LIMIT = population adoption ceiling.
 HONESTY = symmetric cost ledger for policy tradeoffs.
 HONESTY PENALTY = inverse weapon [punishes disclosure].
 Do not conflate these words.
